# Wave 4 stream w4-endings: endings, summary, gallery, night events, side activities

Branch `w4-endings` from main 499b285. Brief: `<scratchpad>/w4_brief.md` (binding). Design: master plan section 5
(Endings, Free-roam activities, Night events), story spec sections 5 and 6.

Priority rule: a playable, tested chain first (T1-T3), then T4, then T5 one activity at a time.

## Interfaces

- **Beat `d7_ending`** (StoryMissions, block `# --- Endings (w4-endings) ---`): kind `cine`, `"ending": "auto"`, no anchor,
  `next` "". Day 7 hands over with `"next": "d7_ending"`.
- **`StoryEndings`** (new, `scripts/story/StoryEndings.gd`): the data of the six endings (id, title, slot sequence, line),
  `slots(x)`, `title(x)`, and the summary rows (`summary(state, ranks)`: choices, E1-E7 found / missing, ranks per mission).
  - A: `end_a`, then `ai_true_end` (the owner's clip `user://video/ai_true_end_m|f.ogv`, else its in-engine fallback).
  - B, D, E: `end_b` / `end_d` / `end_e`. C: `end_c` (Day 5, unchanged). F: `end_a`, `ai_true_end`, `end_f`.
- **StoryDirector** (additive): `ending_state()` (evidence, choices incl. `bribe`, `study_ranks` from `flags.study_ranks`,
  data-driven), `resolve_ending(b)` ("auto" -> `evaluate_ending(ending_state())`), `complete_step` resolves "auto";
  `finish_story` also records the ending in the unlock file (`StoryChapters.record_ending`).
- **StoryRoam** (additive hook): a cine beat with `"ending": "auto"` runs a `StoryEndingRunner` child: each slot in turn
  (video or fallback timeline), then the `StoryEndSummary` screen, then `complete_step` -> `finish_story(X)` -> menu.
- **`StoryEndSummary`** (new CanvasLayer): ending title, the three choices, E1-E7 (missing ones read "Chưa tìm thấy"), the
  best rank per ranked mission, one button VỀ MENU CHÍNH. Plain Vietnamese, no emoji.
- **StoryChapters** (additive): `[endings] reached` in the unlock file (`record_ending`, `reached_endings`, `endings()`);
  the gallery rows of the new ending slots (Day 7). **ChapterSelect**: a third tab KẾT THÚC; **StoryGalleryPlayer**:
  plays a slot sequence (`static var queue`).
- **Night events** (`StoryNightEvents`, additive): the canal ghost boat and the BE6 lift to floor 13, each a second chance
  at a missed optional evidence piece (E3 / E4 at the canal, E2 / E5 at the lift), softened by `GameFX.reduce_scares`.
- **Side activities** (new `scripts/story/side/`): drift races (3 night routes), takedown challenge, delivery run, Vovinam
  street fight; rewards (helmet and jacket colours, S-rank count) in the unlock file. Music slots `side_drift`,
  `side_delivery` (MusicDirector + `docs/cinematics/music_to_download.md`).
