them together; the duel has one arena look all the way.

without it; the whisper and the rain are synthesized SFX), Za Mẫn speaks no real voice, no emoji, no gore, Vietnamese
text, "Giảm hù dọa" softens the monitor face flicker, never stage `.import`, the commit trailer, one commit per task.

**Resume rule:** a later agent resumes from the first unchecked task and the commits on `w3-day5`.

## Tuning target

Sloppy bot, per mission (d5_stealth, d5_gunfight, d5_hack, d5_duel): Thường mean 1-3 fails over 8 seeds and every run done
within 5 retries; Dễ at most 1 fail per run. The good bot passes each on Thường. Re-run once at integration with the
shared gun numbers and bullet time live (shared plan, Integration step 6).
