## Interfaces

- **`StoryScreenCard`** (additive, existing kinds unchanged):
  - kind `"clock"`: a small box in the upper right, between the letterbox bars: `title`, then either a countdown
    (`count`: seconds, ticking down in real time from the card start, red in the last 10) or a flip (`from` -> `to` at
    `flip_u`, the digits fold over); `cine_d3_intro` ("CAMERA LOOP 00:30") and `cine_d7_intro` (07:59 -> 08:00).
  - kind `"terminal"`: a dark terminal window, `title` + `lines` typed out one after another over the window
    (`cine_d5_intro`: the decrypted USB, the basement IP).
  - kind `"cctv"`: optional `zoom` [u_from, u_to, factor] and `focus` (0..1 of the frame): a digital zoom onto the hand-off
    with a "ZOOM xN" tag (`cine_d1_intro`).
  - kind `"flow"`: optional `log` true: scrolling log rows behind the chart (`cine_d5`).
- **`StoryIntroCards`**: new times for the 20 s `ai_intro` (EMAIL 1.4-5.0 typed out over TYPE 1.5-3.4, STAMP_AT 4.0, TEXT
  4.9-8.0, COUNT 8.6-13.0 landing at 10.0, TITLE 13.4-20.0); `state_at` adds `typed` (0..1) and the email body shows that
  share of its characters.
- **`CineAudio.music_offset(slot)`** (new): the choice tails (`CHOICE_TAILS`, a subset of SLOT_FALLBACK) without their
  own owner file start their parent's song at the parent's length (where the parent's music stopped), else 0.
  **`CinePlayer`**: `music_from` (s into the stream), the music plays from it, the fade rule uses the rest of the stream;
  a 0.25 s fade-in when it starts mid-song.
