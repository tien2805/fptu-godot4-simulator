# D2X part B "drift": nitro, spin kick, duck, drift duel with Sói Xám

Date: 2026-10-27. Branch `d2x-drift` (from main fc8701c). Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-day2-tokyo-drift-design.md`,
items D2X-8, D2X-9, D2X-10 and part B of D2X-13. Agent A ("d2x-chase") works in parallel on the chase files; none of them
is touched here.

## Interfaces provided (agent A codes against them with `has_method` guards)

PlayerBike:
- `set_nitro_enabled(on: bool)`, `nitro_enabled: bool` (default false: Stage 1 and Stage 2 never call it)
- `nitro_active() -> bool` (also the nitro-ram flag: touching a biker while it is true is a takedown)
- `drift_charge: float` (0-100), `add_drift_charge(points: float)`
- signals `nitro_started`, `nitro_ended`
- `is_ducking() -> bool` (hold Ctrl while riding)
- `drift_charge_mult: float` (1.0; a drift zone owner sets 1.5 while the bike is inside a zone)
- `apply_speed_profile` unchanged; the nitro bonus sits on top of whatever profile is applied.

Charge sources inside PlayerBike (agent A must not add them again): drifting (25 per second x `drift_charge_mult`),
near misses (+8 each). Takedowns are agent A's: `add_drift_charge(15)`.

PlayerBikeCombat: Q / E while the bike drifts = spin kick. Every target hit goes through the normal path
(`take_kick(away)` + `kick_hit.emit(target, 0)`); `kicked.emit(0)` at the start. Side 0 = a spin. BikeChase reacts through
`BikerEnemy.take_kick` exactly as for a side kick (it does not connect `kick_hit`), so it needs no change.

## Course "Cầu Rạch Hầm" (measured 2026-10-27)

Measurements in the real FreeRoam world (ray + sphere probes on 1-3 m grids; route `BikeRoute.from_points(md.route_points(0.0))`,
bridge middle at s = 2389):
- Nguyễn Xiển north of the bridge and Nguyễn Văn Tăng are 8 m roads with buildings 4-9 m from the centre line: the free
  disc never exceeds 4-8 m radius. The drift turning radius of the bike is 8.4 m (max speed / (steer speed x 1.5)), so a
  U-turn needs a free disc of about 10 m. Đường Số 19 (the only nearby loop) is a 4 m service lane between walls.
- The places with room: the Y junction of Nguyễn Xiển and Nguyễn Văn Tăng (the 114 degree turn, free disc 10 m), the open
  ground where Nguyễn Xiển north-east leaves the buildings (z < -322, past its last lamp posts; open for 80 m and more),
  and the empty south bank east of Nguyễn Xiển (about 75 x 64 m, x 2140-2215, z 168-232).
- A first lap with the hairpin in the small lot by the junction (1.04 km) gave 40-47 s for the bots: too short. The final
  lap runs Nguyễn Xiển north-east to the open ground instead (+300 m).

The lap (1.34 km; `DriftCourse.rach_ham`):
1. Start on the bridge deck at its middle, northbound; Sói Xám 8 m ahead in the left lane, standing beside his bike.
2. Nguyễn Xiển north (320 m) with cone chicane A around z 70-30 (drift zone 1).
3. The Y junction: Nguyễn Văn Tăng is closed by a barrier row with a chevron board; the road bends 61 degrees right
   onto Nguyễn Xiển north-east (drift zone 2), 150 m up between the lamp posts.
4. Hairpin H1, a teardrop on the open ground: a swing right, a left loop of radius 9 around a cone island with a chevron
   board, a swing back onto the southbound lane (drift zone 3).
5. Back through the Y junction, bending left onto Nguyễn Xiển south (drift zone 4), 300 m with the wide S of chicane B in
   the open stretch north of the bridge (drift zone 5).
6. Over the bridge southbound onto the south-bank lot (barriers on its edges and down its middle): a slalom (drift zone 6),
   hairpin H2 round a cone island (drift zone 7), the back straight and the right-hand sweeper onto the bridge (drift
   zone 8), the finish on the deck where the race started.

Why: real roads and lots beside the Rạch Hầm bridge, the real junction geometry, the start and finish where the chase
ends; the U-turns only where the world has room for the bike's real turning circle (every sample of the line keeps 1.2 m
clear on both sides in the real world, a test checks it); 8 drift zones (the spec asks for at least 5). The 114 degree
corner is used as the entry of the junction bends rather than as a full turn: Nguyễn Văn Tăng has no room for a U-turn.

## Tuning (test bots, `test_drift_duel` part B, 2026-10-27)

Bots ride the real PlayerBike in FreeRoam with Input actions: they steer for the line 3.5-13 m ahead, hold the rival's
profile speed x k, drift in the zones' corners, fire nitro on straights.

| Bot | Result | Lap | Points | Tailing | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| good (k 1.3, drifts every corner under 30 m, nitro) | won on points, no reset | 55.2 s | 3123 | 40 s | -6..15 m |
| medium (k 1.05, drifts only under 16 m) | lost on points | 59.6 s | 949 | 15 s | 7..19 m |
| slow (13 m/s, never drifts) | never dropped (60 s window) | - | 0 | 2 s | 7..38 m |
| stops dead at 140 m | lost on the gap | 11.8 s | 0 | - | 7..52 m |

Resulting values:
- Duel profile 24 m/s (86 km/h), accel 13.5, drag 0.014; nitro +8 on top. At the chase profile (27.8 m/s) the first lap
  took 40-43 s.
- Rival: top 23.5 m/s, lateral grip 20 m/s^2 (30 while the player is on his tail or ahead: he drifts harder), braking 14,
  acceleration 12 (as quick off the line as the player); full-pace lap 61 s; drifts about 8-10 s per lap, nitro bursts
  out of the tight corners onto the long straights.
- Rubber band: x1.2 when passed, x1.06 under 4 m, x1.03 under 12 m, x1.0 to 22 m, then down to x0.15 at 38 m (a rider
  still moving never drops past 40 m; only a stop or a reset loses on the gap). Grace 3 s after a reset.
- Points: 10 per (m/s x s) drifting in a zone, x2 within 12 m. Target 1600: about half of the good bot, well over the
  medium bot, so a first try that drifts only the hairpins fails and drifting the chicanes, the slalom and the bends
  while tailing wins.
- Overtake: 3 m ahead for 4 s. With the fight profile and x1.2 it takes a nitro on a straight or a clearly better drift
  line; the good bot did it once in an earlier tuning run (before the fight profile), not in the final runs.
- Quick retry: 1.2 s on the loss line, a 0.2 s fade, a 2 s countdown (3.4 s to the next GO).
- Cost: the duel's own tick 0.21-0.24 ms median (rival, gates, points, cones, HUD), the whole physics frame 0.6-0.8 ms in
  FreeRoam without the crowd.
