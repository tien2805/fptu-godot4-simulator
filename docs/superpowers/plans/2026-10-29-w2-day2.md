## Course facts (measured 2026-10-29 in FreeRoam, `w2d2/probe_course.gd`, `w2d2/loops.py`)

- No road loop passes the Rạch Hầm bridge (the creek has one crossing). The nearest real loop of lit roads is
  Nguyễn Văn Tăng -> Đỗ Thế Diện -> Đường số 20 (672 m, all "corridor" region, 6-8 m roads, lamps 4-21 m off the line,
  ±3-4 m free all round). Nguyễn Xiển from the bridge to the Y junction and Nguyễn Văn Tăng to the loop are lit 8 m roads
  (no lamps within 45 m of the bridge: the cine-stage clearance; the neon arches and flares light it).
- **The race "Rạch Hầm GP"** (one open path, `DriftCourse.rach_ham()`): grid on the bridge deck northbound -> Nguyễn Xiển
  north (320 m) -> the Y junction (114 degree left onto Nguyễn Văn Tăng) -> the lap line at Đường số 20 -> **2 laps** of the
  loop (Nguyễn Văn Tăng west 310 m, the hairpin right onto Đỗ Thế Diện, 290 m, right onto số 20, right back onto Nguyễn Văn
  Tăng) -> left out of the loop after lap 2 -> the Y junction (114 degree right) -> Nguyễn Xiển south -> the finish line
  mid-deck on the bridge. About 2.1 km, 9 drift corners. Each lap with its run to and from the bridge is about 1.07 km
  (the master plan's "2 laps of about 1.2 km" is met as closely as the real roads allow, with no open-ground cones).

## Interfaces

- `DriftCourse` (open path, no wrap): `pts / cum / length`, `point(s)`, `tangent(s)`, `right(s)`, `world(s, d)`,
  `yaw_at(s)`, `half_width_at(s)`, `project_near(p, s_hint)`, `project_any(p, s_hint)` (windowed), `lap_s` (lap 1 start,
  lap 2 start, loop exit), `lap_at(s)`, `corners` ([s0, s1, dir, label]), `corner_at(s)`, `gates` (reset points),
  `arches` / `arrows` / `crowds` (prop data), rival `speed_at(s, fight)`, `radius_at(s)`.
- `DriftRival` = the player's kick target (PlayerBikeCombat duck-typing): `kickable()`, `take_kick(away, power)`,
  `take_counter(away)`, `time_to_hit()`, `ground_pos()`, `hp` (kicks left to the wobble), `impact_react(tier, dir, opts)`.
  Race AI: lateral line `d` (racing line, block, attack), kicks (`kick_hit` through `duel.rival_kick()`), nitro charges,
  wobble (`WOBBLE_S` = 2 s, speed x `WOBBLE_SPEED`).
- `DriftDuel` keeps its signals (`countdown_started`, `music_cue`, `go`, `won(how)`, `lost(reason)`, `ended`) and adds
  `lap_started(i)`; `won` how = "race"; `lost` reason = "beaten" | "knocked"; `retry()` restarts on the grid or, once the
  player reached lap 2, on the lap 2 grid (a fair checkpoint). `combat` (PlayerBikeCombat), `props` (DriftRaceProps).
- New `DriftRaceProps` (scripts/combat/bike/): neon arches, yellow road arrows and chevron boards, the Bầy Sói pack (one
  MultiMesh crowd with flares, cheering bob, a few pooled lights and smoke near the player), fireworks at the finish.
- `BikeSetPieces` kinds: `melons` (truck tips over in slow motion, 100 pooled melons), `barrier` (now a crane lowering a
  steel beam with sparks, [CTRL] or the kerb gap), `ramp` (now a broken road gap, a real jump with the director's slow
  motion orbit), `roadblock` (new: police cars across the road, thread the gap), `wrong_way`, `drift`, `police` (kept,
  no longer in the Day 2 layout). Each big one opens with `CameraDirector.preview_cut(target, 0.6)`.
- `BikerEnemy.impact_react(tier, dir, opts)` per kind; `BikeCombatData.kind(k)` returns the difficulty-adjusted kind.
- Shared file (minimal): `PlayerBikeCombat` picks the Impact tier from the kick's result (a downing kick = finisher)
  and passes the target so `impact_react` runs.

## Tuning (sloppy bot: 250-400 ms reaction, 15 % wrong or late inputs, imperfect telegraph reading, hits traffic)

Targets: Thường 1-3 fails per mission and done within 5 retries; Dễ at most 1 fail.

Harnesses (FreeRoam at night, `--fixed-fps 60`, about 15-30 s real per mission):
- `tests/sim_drift_race.gd -- <normal|easy> <seed> <missions>`: the race with the sloppy bot; the race retries by itself
  (grid / lap 2 grid); a mission ends on the win (or 6 fails).
- `tests/sim_bike_chase_d2.gd -- sloppy <seed> <runs> <normal|easy>`: the whole chase (waves, set pieces, cameo) with
  THỬ LẠI after every knock-off.
- The sloppy bot (both): a 250-400 ms delay on every decision (+0.3 s when late), 15 % wrong (dodge the wrong way, kick
  the wrong side, no duck, a roadblock gap missed, a drift dropped or held too long), reads 65 % of the telegraphs, a slow
  lateral wander off the line (it meets traffic in the chase), a 0.1 s steering lag.
- Note: GameFX loads the owner's difficulty at startup; both harnesses set `Difficulty` afterwards.
