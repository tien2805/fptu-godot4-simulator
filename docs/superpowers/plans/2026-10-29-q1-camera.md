# Q1 Camera director: plan

Date: 2026-10-29. Branch `q-cam` (from main abded62). Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-quality-overhaul-and-phase-d-plan.md`,
section 2 (item 5) and Q1. No screenshots: the framing tests replace them.

## Shape

- One director node per scene, `CameraDirector` (group `camera_director`, static `CameraDirector.current`). It owns the
  shared camera effects and the director cuts. Every camera owner installs it on demand (`CameraDirector.ensure(node)`,
  deferred, into the current scene), so FreeRoam, the stages and the tests get one without edits.
- The gameplay cameras keep their nodes (`player.camera`, `player.cam_pivot`, `player.cam_ray`, `bike.chase_cam`,
  `bike.spring_arm`, `chase.cam`): many systems call `make_current()` on them or add camera exceptions to them. Their maths
  moves into rigs under `scripts/camera/` that follow one rule book.
- Reference values live in one place (`CameraRules`). Motion uses an exact critically damped spring (`CamSpring`).
- The same framing code (`CameraFraming`) picks the director's and the takedown camera angles at runtime and checks the
  framing in the tests.

## Interfaces

`CameraDirector` (other streams reach it through `get_tree().get_first_node_in_group("camera_director")` + `has_method`):
- `punch(strength: float, dir: Vector3 = Vector3.ZERO)`: an impact kick (offset along dir in camera space, a small
  rotation and an FOV dip, springing back). On the bike, a punch with a dir is also the 0.12 s whip toward it.
- `shake(trauma: float)`: trauma adds up to 1, decays at a fixed rate in real time, amplitude = trauma squared, smooth noise.
- `preview_cut(target: Node3D, secs: float = 0.6)`: a director camera shows the target (angle picked by occlusion test),
  slow motion through `RadialWheel.scale_owner` while it runs (never over another owner), then the previous camera.
- `slowmo_orbit(target: Node3D, secs: float = 0.5)`: slow motion plus an orbit round the target (clear side picked by
  occlusion test), then the previous camera and normal time.
- `fx_offset`, `fx_rot_deg`, `fx_fov`: what the rigs add on top of their own pose. A camera without the `director_fx`
  meta gets the shake through `GameFX.shake` instead.
- `cutting() -> bool`, `cut_kind`, `cam` (the director camera, made lazily).

Rigs (owned by their camera owners, no autoload names):
- `FootCamRig` for `ThirdPersonController` (`player.cam_rig`); `ThirdPersonController.shake(amp)` keeps working.
- `BikeCamRig` for `PlayerBike` (`bike.cam_rig`); `add_camera_shake(amount)` keeps working; new `cam_whip(toward)`.
- `CineShots` new rigs and `CineTimeline` helpers: `two_shot`, `ots_shot`, `hero_shot` (the existing low_hero), `dutch_shot`,
  `push_in`. Existing timelines are unchanged; a rig may now return a `roll`, which CinePlayer applies.

## Reference values (CameraRules)

On foot: shoulder 0.45 m right, distance 3.2 m, pivot height 1.55 m, critically damped follow (omega 14, vertical 9),
look-ahead 0.8 m in the move direction, sprint FOV +6, landing dip 0.08 / 0.16 / 0.24 m (soft / roll / splat),
collision pull-in by probes through `cam_ray` (its exception list is kept): a shoulder probe, a centre probe and two
side probes 0.2 m out, plus rays between the player's head / hips and the camera spot (both ways), a 0.25 m margin, fast
in, slow out. Added while testing on the real map: close in the shoulder offset gives way and the camera cranes up; a
knocked-down player gets a body-centred pivot and a 0.9 m high angle; a framing guard turns the camera just enough to
keep head / chest / hips inside; the shoulder view converges on the first body or wall straight ahead (hip-fire line).

Melee and sword combat: focus 70/30 between the player and the target; distance 3.5 m for one enemy within 8 m, +1 m per
extra enemy up to 6.5 m; auto-yaw at most 90 deg/s toward the yaw that frames the target and the next attacker (an enemy
winding up within 75 deg of the view; attackers behind keep the HUD edge markers), only after 0.5 s without mouse look;
the same pull-in, so the camera never ends inside walls; the focus shift never leaves the camera over the player; with
the back to a wall (< 1.8 m room) the auto-yaw aims at a 70 deg side view; the guard keeps the target in frame too.

Bike: camera 1.35 m above the road, distance 3.8 m at rest to 5.2 m at top speed, FOV 70 to 86 with speed, +8 with nitro
(the nitro punch stays), roll 30 % of the lean, drift swing 12-18 deg to the outside, kick whip 0.12 s (7 deg) toward
the target, road shake growing with speed and kept small (smooth noise), yaw locked to the bike plus a sprung offset.
The SpringArm3D stays for the collision pull-in (sphere 0.2 m, its excluded objects kept).

Takedown cams: 3 candidates (side, front-low, high-rear) relative to the falling rider; the first with a clear view and
not in geometry wins; re-checked at 10 Hz.

Framing: safe area = 8 % margins (inside the letterbox for cinematics); subject points = head centre, chest and hips
(skeleton bones, fixed heights as fallback); line of sight = no static geometry (StaticBody3D, not AnimatableBody3D) on the
ray; camera not in geometry = clear line from the subject and no static overlap within 0.12 m.

## Tasks

1. **Core helpers**: `scripts/camera/CamSpring.gd`, `CameraRules.gd`, `CameraFraming.gd`. Test `tests/test_camera_director.gd`
   part U: no overshoot and settling of the spring, every mapping at its ends, framing maths on a known camera.
2. **CameraDirector**: `scripts/camera/CameraDirector.gd`. Part D: group, `ensure` installs one, trauma squared falloff, punch
   decays, preview cut and slow-motion orbit frame the target (a wall blocks one candidate), respect another time-scale
   owner, give the camera and the time scale back, GameFX fallback for other cameras.
3. **On-foot rig**: `scripts/camera/FootCamRig.gd`, the camera part of `scripts/ThirdPersonController.gd`. Part F (arena):
   shoulder, distance, height, look-ahead, sprint FOV, landing dip, pull-in against a wall behind and to the right,
   never inside a wall, framing at every check.
4. **Combat framing** in `FootCamRig`. Part C (arena, a MeleeFight with 1-4 enemies): distance by enemy count, the focus
   point, both player and target in the safe area with a clear view, the yaw rate never above 90 deg/s, the "behind the
   camera" case of test_melee_combat still behind.
5. **Bike rig**: `scripts/camera/BikeCamRig.gd`, `_update_chase_camera` of `scripts/PlayerBike.gd`. Part B (arena): height,
   distance and FOV by speed, nitro, roll, drift swing, whip, shake stays small, rider framed at every speed.
6. **Takedown cams**: `_cam_*` of `scripts/combat/bike/BikeChase.gd` (3 candidates, the director-cut-safe restore, the kick
   whip hooked on the ChaseCam setup). Part T (arena): walls block candidates one by one, the picked one frames the rider.
7. **Cinematic shot helpers**: `scripts/cine/CineShots.gd`, `CineTimeline.gd`, `CinePlayer.gd` (roll). Part S: every helper
   frames its subjects inside the letterboxed safe area at u = 0, 0.5, 1; validate() accepts them; the roll is applied.
8. **Real-map framing test** `tests/test_camera_framing.gd` (FreeRoam): on foot at spots sampled next to buildings and palm
   trunks (wall behind, beside, ahead), walking past them, melee with 1-4 enemies at sampled spots, the bike at speed and
   in a drift on real roads, a BikeChase on Nguyễn Xiển with takedown cams over the bridge. Every sample: head and torso
   in the safe area, line of sight clear, camera not in geometry, in combat both player and target framed.
9. **Regression and perf**: the tests touching these files, smoke, `--check-only`; `test_combat_perf` and `test_bike_perf`
   alone on AC power.
