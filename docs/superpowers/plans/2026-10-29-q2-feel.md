## Shared rules

- Every hit goes through one recipe, `Impact.hit(ctx, tier, opts)`, tiers `light` / `heavy` / `finisher`.
- Engine.time_scale is written only through `RadialWheel.scale_owner`. The Impact clock is one node (group `impact_clock`,
  process mode always) that owns hit-stops and combat slow motion; a hit-stop is skipped while anyone else owns the time
  scale; set-piece slow motion (BikeChase) and the wheel take over from a running hit-stop (`Impact.yield_scale()`).
- Real time = physics frames (1 / physics_ticks_per_second), so tests count frames.
- Camera: the Q1 node in group `camera_director` (`punch`, `shake`, `slowmo_orbit`) through `has_method`; fallback
  GameFX.shake on foot, PlayerBike.add_camera_shake on the bike.
- Particles: the Q3 node in group `fx_pool` (`spawn(kind, pos, dir, scale)`); fallback WeaponFX bursts.
- Sound: synthesized SFX only (`ImpactAudio`, worker-thread warm-up, cached). No music.
- No camera or look files are edited (ThirdPersonController camera, PlayerBike chase cam, BikeChase `_cam_*`,
  `scripts/camera/`, GameFX environment, ViceStyle, LookPresets, `scripts/fx/`).

## Tuning values (starting point)

| Tier | Hit-stop | Punch | Knockback | Particles | Sound layers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| light | 40 ms | 0.15 | 0.3 m flinch | dust | whoosh + thud |
| heavy | 80 ms | 0.35 | 1.5 m stagger | dust ring + sparks | + body + crowd "ồ" |
| finisher | 150 ms | 0.6 | tumble ~3 m, floor bounce, stars | ring, sparks, debris | + meme sting |

- Hit-stop scale 0.05. Light hit-stops: at most 60 ms granted within any 0.2 s window (no stutter lock); several hits in one
  frame take the longest, never the sum.
- Last-enemy knockout: after the hit-stop, 0.5 s real at 0.3x plus `slowmo_orbit`.
- Crowd "ồ" at most every 1.2 s, the sting at most every 1 s; one copy of a cue per frame.

### T2 Melee (PlayerCombat, MeleeEnemy, MeleeFight)
- Strikes use the tiers: jab / cross light; elbow, heavy kick, counter, guard break, knockdown, slam heavy; finisher and KO
  finisher. The finisher's 0.3 s slow motion runs after its hit-stop on the clock.
- MeleeEnemy: `impact_react` picks a directional reaction (front / back / left / right; head or body) from the pack clips
  (HitRecieve, HitRecieve_2) plus a directional code-pose lean; knockback by tier; KO = comic flying tumble (spin, floor
  bounce, stars).
- Environmental slams: a knockback whose path meets a wall, table or car (ray at 10 Hz budget: only on hits) slams the enemy
  into it: heavy tier, extra damage, a slam sound by surface (group `slam_table`, `slam_car` / vehicle, else wall).
- Throws: [E] on a staggered enemy within 1.5 m (no dazed enemy near): grab and throw 3.5 m along the input / facing; a
  throw into a wall or another enemy is a slam (both fall).
- Improvised weapons `scripts/combat/ImprovisedWeapon.gd`: chair (4 hits), tray (3), helmet (3), trash-can lid (5); [E]
  picks one up within 1.6 m; held in the right hand; light / heavy swings hit harder; each landed hit costs one; at 0 a
  comic break (pieces fly, a break sound, popup). MeleeFight opt `props` and `add_prop(kind, at)`.
- Test `tests/test_melee_feel.gd`.

### T3 Bike (PlayerBikeCombat, BikerEnemy, BikeChase hit and crash parts only)
- Kick connect: heavy tier (hit-stop, camera whip + punch toward the target), the target's wobble on a decaying curve with a
  flailing rider pose, then a crash tumble with flying parts (mirror, helmet) and the bike sliding with sparks; a small
  kick-back shove on the player's bike.
- Counter-kick "PHẢN ĐÒN!": a kick toward an attacker whose hit is at most 0.25 s away sends him flying (crash cause
  `counter`, a takedown), short slow motion, no damage to the player.
- Grab weapon: a stick rider downed by a kick drops his stick on the road; riding over it picks it up: 3 swings on Q / E,
  reach +1.0 m, heavy hits; then it breaks.
- Spin kick: a fading motion-trail arc behind the leg; its hits use the finisher tier when two connect.
- Hits on the player: red screen-edge flash, a camera jolt, a 0.5 s bike wobble.
- Test `tests/test_bike_feel.gd`.
