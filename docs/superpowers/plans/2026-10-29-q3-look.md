# Q3 "Look": look presets, readability, VFX library, night roads

Date: 2026-10-29. Branch `q-look`. Master plan: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-quality-overhaul-and-phase-d-plan.md` (Q3).

No screenshots or preview renders: every value is a conservative reference value, checked by headless tests
(values applied and restored, counts and budgets, shader compile). The owner playtests at the end.

## Rules for every task
- Layer on ViceStyle and the GameFX quality levels, never replace them: a preset edits a shallow copy of the environment
  that ViceStyle (or the scene) built, and is re-applied when ViceStyle rebuilds it.
- No per-frame allocation; CPUParticles3D only; code-built meshes and textures only.
- Files owned by other streams are not edited, except one helper line per enemy build (listed in the report).

## Task 1: LookPresets
Files: `scripts/LookPresets.gd` (new, class_name), `scripts/LookZone.gd` (new), `scripts/GameFX.gd` (one refresh call at the
end of `upgrade_scene_visuals`), `scripts/FreeRoam.gd` (base preset per time of day), `MissionBikeChase.gd` /
`MissionDriftDuel.gd` (apply on start, restore on cleanup), `LairYard.gd` (a LookZone), `StoryBasement.gd` (base preset).

Interface:
- `LookPresets.apply(name, env_owner)` pushes a preset on the owner's stack and renders; `restore(env_owner, name = "")`
  pops it (or the top); `set_base(env_owner, name)` sets the bottom layer ("" = none); `refresh(env_owner)` re-renders
  after the scene rebuilt its environment; `active(env_owner)`; `PRESETS`, `names()`.
- Render: the owner's first WorldEnvironment gets a shallow copy of its base environment with the preset's exposure,
  glow (intensity, strength, HDR threshold, luminance cap, bloom; quality 0 drops the two widest glow levels), depth fog
  (colour, begin, end, density), adjustments (brightness, contrast, saturation) and ambient. The first DirectionalLight3D
  is the key (colour, energy, shadow opacity); a shadowless "LookFill" DirectionalLight3D is the fill. No preset = the
  base environment and the key's own values come back, the fill hides.
- Presets: night_city, tokyo_drift, port_night, server_red, canal_night, penthouse, campus_day.
- Wiring: FreeRoam night -> night_city base, morning / noon -> campus_day base, dawn / sunset -> none; Day 2 chase and
  duel push tokyo_drift; the Day 3 yard has a LookZone (port_night within 110 m, off past 150 m, 2 Hz check); the Day 5
  basement sets server_red; canal_night and penthouse wait for Phase D.

Tests: `tests/test_look_presets.gd`: every preset applies its values, stack push / pop restores the layer below and then
the base exactly, set_base, refresh after an environment rebuild, the fill light, the LookZone hysteresis, FreeRoam-like
owner without a WorldEnvironment does not error.

## Task 2: Readability
Files: `scripts/Readability.gd` (new: rim shader, telegraph colours), `scripts/GameFX.gd` (setting `video/rim_light`,
default on, CheckButton "Viền sáng nhân vật" in `build_style_settings`, synced in `sync_style_settings`), one line each in
MeleeEnemy (melee, Dolly clones, the sword boss), BikerEnemy, Gunman, AmmoPickup; telegraph colours in BikeSetPieces.

Interface: `Readability.rim(root, kind = "enemy", shell = false)` (kind enemy red-orange / pickup yellow; overlay pass, or
child shell meshes where the owner uses material_overlay itself), `set_enabled(on)`, `enabled()`, `rim_material(kind)`,
colours `DANGER`, `PROMPT`, `ZONE`, `telegraph_material(kind, energy)`.

Tests: `tests/test_readability.gd`: shader compiles (uniform list, no SHADER ERROR in the log), rim on a built
character (overlay and shell modes, Label3D skipped), toggle off / on, GameFX setting saved to a temp path and the
CheckButton present in both settings panels.

## Task 3: VFX library
Files: `scripts/fx/FxPool.gd` (new, class_name), installed in FreeRoam, StoryBasement and NightBuilding.

Interface: `FxPool.spawn(kind, pos, dir = Vector3.UP, scale = 1.0) -> bool`, `find(tree) -> FxPool` (group "fx_pool"),
`active_count(kind = "")`, `active_particles()`, `budget(kind)`, `KINDS`. Kinds: sparks, dust, dust_ring, smoke, debris,
splash, fire, rain_burst, speed_lines, nitro_flame. Each kind has a fixed number of prebuilt emitters (budget) with
shared meshes and materials; spawning past the budget reuses the oldest emitter. Quality 0 halves particle amounts.

Tests: `tests/test_fx_pool.gd`: group join, every kind spawns, budget never exceeded, reuse of the oldest, no new nodes
after warm-up, continuous kinds stop on time, total particle cap.

## Task 4: Night roads
Files: `scripts/roam/NightRoads.gd` (new), FreeRoam (build + toggle in `set_time_of_day`).

- Street lamps every ~34 m, staggered on both kerbs of every carriageway drawn in free roam (not service, not bridges,
  not inside the campus plot, only where the road is drawn), deduplicated.
- Per 160 m cell: one MultiMesh of posts (always), one of lamp heads (emissive, night only) and one of light pools
  (shared unshaded additive radial quads like the traffic LampPool, night only, visibility range with fade).
- A pool of 4 OmniLights (no shadows) moved to the lamps nearest the camera at 4 Hz, night only.
- Night: lane paint brighter (night_line materials), POI sign plates glow; both back to normal by day and on exit.

Tests: `tests/test_night_roads.gd`: lamps built on a real region, pools / heads / lights visible at night only, the light
pool never exceeds 4 and follows the camera, lane and sign materials restored, render budget (draw instances, lights).
