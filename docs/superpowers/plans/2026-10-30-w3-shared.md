commit trailer, one commit per task.

## Owner constraints that bind this stream

- The owner plays once at the end. Reviews, tests and the sloppy bot replace playtests.
- Thường is hard (a first run fails 1-3 times), and Dễ exists. Retries are instant from a fair checkpoint.
- Every hit goes through the Q2 Impact recipe. The time scale is written only through `RadialWheel.scale_owner`.
- The quality bar (section 2) applies to every gunfight that gets bullet time.

## Sloppy gun bot (the model all three gun harnesses copy)

250-400 ms reaction on every decision (+0.3 s when late); 15 % wrong or late (stays popped out too long, picks a cover
that does not protect, flanks the wrong way, reloads in the open, a QTE or prompt pressed late); reads 65 % of the muzzle
glints; a shaky aim (hit chance about 55 % at 15 m standing, worse moving); uses a dive at most once per 20 s when the
focus is full and 3+ enemies are up.

Made precise in T11 (the harness header has the full model; the day harnesses copy it): finding a target after popping up
or switching is a decision (it waits the reaction); the bot's clock is real time and its fire cadence stays one round per
0.25 real s inside a dive; a flanker who shoots it while crouched makes it move to a slot that also covers him; out of
its slot after a dive it takes the slot again; it never snaps into a slot from further than `COVER_ENTER_R`.

Final gun row (Thường / Dễ): telegraph 0.35 / 0.45 s (unchanged: fairness), shooters 4 / 3, regeneration delay 7 / 3 s,
accuracy x2.0 / x0.8, damage 16 / 8 HP a bullet; retry mercy 0.15 per loss in a row on a wave, floor 0.55 (both
difficulties). Thường is harder because a shot that resolves on an exposed player lands more often and hurts more, never
because the glint is shorter: a player who reads the glints and ducks still takes no damage.

What the resume found (the earlier "mean 0.6-0.75" was an artifact):
- the harness set the bot's timers before resetting its clock, so in every run after the first the bot sat in cover for
  ~100 s without shooting until it died: the "one fail per run, then a clean retry" pattern; the dive counters were never
  reset either (dives stopped after run 1), and the bot fired 2.9x faster in a dive;
- the "easy" runs played on the owner's saved difficulty (GameFX loads the settings in `_ready`, after the harness set
  Dễ): the earlier "Dễ on target" was Thường; the harness now sets the difficulty after the first frame and prints the row;
- after a dive the bot believed it was back in cover while lying in the open (it left the dive state before BulletTime
  started the dive), and it sat in a slot a flanker was shooting into.
With those fixed, the plan's row gave Thường a mean of 0 (2-6 hits for the whole fight). Trials, in order (Thường, no
dives unless noted): heavier waves alone 0.00; + damage 12: 0.25; regen 7: 0.50; gunmen holding their aim longer
(`PEEK_ABORT_S` 2.5), faster peeks and bursts of 2-3: no change (enemy fire is limited by line of sight: gunmen can only
shoot during the bot's pop-outs, and it ducks on 65 % of the glints); accuracy is the lever that scales hits (x1.6 /
damage 16 / regen 7: 1.13, but dives 0.25); x1.8-2.0 reached the mean but some runs chained 5-6 losses on wave 3 (each
retry an independent ~60 % death chance), so the retry mercy was added: x2.0 then met every target. The cadence trials
were reverted (only the row, the damage entry and the mercy are kept).
