# Plan: Day 2 "Tokyo Drift" chase (agent A, branch d2x-chase)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-day2-tokyo-drift-design.md`, items D2X-1 to D2X-7, D2X-11, D2X-12 and the agent A part
of D2X-13. Agent B owns PlayerBike, PlayerBikeCombat, the nitro gauge, the drift duel, StoryMissions / StoryRoam and
chapter select; this branch never edits those files.

## Route facts (measured 2026-10-27, `BikeRoute.from_points(md.route_points(0.0))`)

- 5476 m long; the Rạch Hầm bridge middle at s 2389; Anh Duy's corner projects onto s 0 (106 m off the route).
- Turn centres (deg over +/-15 m): 0 (79), 40 (89), 500 / 520 (91, 85), 760 (75), 920 / 940 / 960 (-57, -129, 70),
  1280 (92), 1420 / 1440 (-89, -80), 2280 (94, onto Nguyễn Xiển).
- Straights: Hải Đăng 60-500, N2 620-760 and 800-920, Anh Đào 5 / Cầu Vồng 3 960-1280, Cầu Vồng 5 1460-2260 (800 m),
  Nguyễn Xiển 2280-2389. Every road is 6 m wide (2 lanes, two-way) except Nguyễn Xiển (8 m).
- So the long "boulevard" of the spec is Cầu Vồng 5, not Nguyễn Xiển (only the last 110 m): the cameo, the barrier and the
  police sit on Cầu Vồng 5.

## Interfaces

- **B's bike interface**, all guarded with `has_method` and overridable by Callables for the tests:
  - `BikeChase.duck_fn` / `nitro_fn` / `charge_fn` (default: `bike.is_ducking()`, `bike.nitro_active()`,
    `bike.add_drift_charge(points)`);
  - `set_nitro_enabled(true)` at the chase start when the opt `nitro` is set.
- **Spin kick:** `PlayerBikeCombat.kick_hit(target, 0)`. Two spin hits within 0.3 s tag both riders "spin", so their crash
  is a takedown.
- **BikeChase.setup opts (new):**
  - `profile` (default `PlayerBike.STAGE_PROFILE`; Day 2 `BikeCombatData.CHASE_PROFILE`);
  - `opening` {drop_s, scale};
  - `music_pos_fn` (-> playback seconds, < 0 = no music, then a real-time timer);
  - `set_pieces` (data list);
  - `cameo` {s, force_s, body_fn, card_fn};
  - `nitro`, `takedown_cam`.
- **add_wave(spawns, wave_opts):** wave opts {max_attackers, force_s, after_cameo}. Spawn "from": "side" bursts out beside
  the player, out of view. Spawn opts "kind": rider | stick | thrower | heavy | pickup.
- **New signals:**
  - `takedown(biker, cause)`;
  - `set_piece(kind, phase)`;
  - `cameo_started` / `cameo_done`;
  - `opening_drop`.

## Tasks

1. **Data, route, speed** (BikeCombatData, BikeChase, MissionBikeChase, BikeRoute).
   - Kinds table, chase profile 27.8 / 30.6 m/s, biker top speed 32 (heavy 29.5), 3 attackers (4 in W3) at least 0.6 s
     apart, rider kick 22.
   - No regeneration; a cleared wave gives 25 back; traffic crashes cost 15-25 by speed.
   - `day2_route` = route_points(0.0); `BikeRoute.turn_deg`.
   - Tests: data, route, profile, caps.
2. **Enemy types** (BikerEnemy, BikeChase).
   - Stick: 0.5 s raised wind-up, the speed locked 0.35 s before the swing, so braking or steering dodges; a kick
     interrupts.
   - Thrower: two riders; the pillion throws a helmet in an arc; a red road marker 0.8 s ahead; it drops back
     alongside after each throw (the kick window).
   - Heavy: 3 kicks, a 30 dmg shoulder ram.
   - Pickup: cannot be kicked; a 1.0 s horn and headlight telegraph; the speed is locked 0.5 s before the ram; two dodged
     rams, or a car, and it crashes ("pole").
   - Police: rams bikers and the player, cannot be kicked.
   - Tests: each telegraph and counter.
3. **Slow motion and takedowns** (BikeChase, BikeHUD).
   - Every time-scale write goes through `RadialWheel.scale_owner`, and the time scale is restored on stop, retry, lost,
     won and exit.
   - Takedown causes: traffic, pole, melon, barrier, police, nitro, spin, air.
   - Effect: 0.5 s at 0.3x, a side camera cut, the popup rotation, the HUD counter, +15 charge.
   - Rate limit: at most one every 3 s, and none while 2+ attackers are winding up within 6 m.
   - Tests.
4. **Film opening** (BikeChase, BikeHUD).
   - 0.35x until `OPEN_DROP_S` = 4.15 s. The drop follows the music position when `music_pos_fn` reports one, else a
     real-time timer.
   - A low camera with letterbox and the start line; the player steers.
   - At the drop: time back to 1, a shake, W1 bursts from the sides (LED eyes flare, horns).
   - Tests: timer path, music path (stub), time scale.
5. **Set pieces** (new `BikeSetPieces.gd`), a data list keyed by route distance, each with a telegraph, re-armed on retry:
   - melons (a truck with hazard lights, the tailgate drops, 24 pooled MultiMesh melons for 5 s, 10 dmg, bikers crash);
   - barrier (signs 80 m before, "[CTRL] CÚI XUỐNG", duck or the curb gap, else knocked off);
   - wrong way (cones and flashing arrows close the right half for 250 m, the AI clamps to the left half, cones 8 dmg);
   - ramp (above 20 m/s: a 1.2 s jump at 0.4x, alongside bikers airborne, air kick = takedown);
   - police (2 cops for 25 s, dropping back before the Nguyễn Xiển turn);
   - drift zones (chevrons and "DRIFT!"; fast without a drift = curb 10 dmg and a slowdown; drifting = 1.5x charge).
   - Tests: trigger at distance, re-arm, barrier with the duck stub, police event.
6. **Cameo and story wiring** (new `BikeCameo.gd`, MissionBikeChase, MissionBridge, MusicDirector).
   - Sói Xám rides up behind, wheelies past; the villain card (moved from MissionBridge); the taunt; the blue nitro flame;
     away; then W3.
   - Music:
     - the chase plays `mission_bike_chase` with no crossfade, warmed first;
     - W3 crossfades to `mission_bike_chase_b` when that file exists;
     - the replay uses no slot, so the song never restarts.
   - Tests: the chase part of `test_story_day2`.
7. **Tuning and perf.**
   - `tests/sim_bike_chase_d2.gd` plays the real route with a "first-timer" bot and a "skilled" bot (fixed fps).
   - `test_bike_perf` covers set pieces, melons and police.
   - The results go in the table below.

## Tuning results

Bots: `tests/sim_bike_chase_d2.gd`, the real route at night in FreeRoam, `--fixed-fps 60`.
- **first:** reacts to a telegraph 55 % of the time after 0.38 s, kicks after 0.3 s with 70 % odds, ducks 70 %, drifts 30 %.
- **skilled:** reacts 90 % after 0.2 s, kicks at once, always ducks, always drifts.
- **Both:** a knock-off retries at the checkpoint after 1.5 s, like THỬ LẠI.

| Round | Change | first (fails per run) | skilled |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | the first numbers | 2.67 (1, 1, 6) | 0, 106 s |
| 1 | flankers pace out of kick reach; police 12 dmg; pickup at most 3 rams | 2.25 | 0 |
| 2 | barrier moved after the W3 start; checkpoint at the cameo | 0.25 | 0 |
| 3 | W2 as reinforcements; quicker pacing; thrower back in reach sooner | 1.50 | 0 |
| 4-5 | lighter W3; quicker cameo; police player cooldown; pickup back to 35 | 0.75-0.88 | 0-0.25 |
| final | thrower top 33 m/s, the W3 thrower spawns first | **1.13** (8 runs: 3, 0, 1, 0, 1, 1, 1, 2) | **0** (4 runs) |

What each round fixed:
- **Round 0:**
  - Flankers paced inside kick reach, so kicks were free kills: W1 was cleared in 12 s.
  - Most fails came in W3 with the police: police rams of 18 aimed at the player 35 % of the time, and unlimited pickup rams.
  - Throwers threw about once per run.
- **Round 1:**
  - Flank slots moved to 2.9-3.2 m. The kick reach is 2.8 m, so a kick is now a counter in the wind-up, or a steer toward the rider.
  - Police: 12 dmg, at the player 20 % of the time.
  - Pickup: 30 dmg, at most 3 rams.
  - The barrier at s 1720 was still hit during the cameo, before the W3 checkpoint, so each retry went back to W2.
- **Round 2:**
  - Barrier moved to s 1950; the cameo start is a checkpoint.
  - This left a 30 s lull: W2 was cleared at about 27 s and the cameo came at about 61 s.
- **Round 3:**
  - W2 arrives as reinforcements over ~20 s (delays 0 / 1 / 7 / 13 / 19 s).
  - PACE 0.9-2.2 s; the thrower paces 7.5 m ahead and drops back for 2.2 s.
  - W3 was never cleared before the bridge.
- **Rounds 4-5:**
  - W3 riders and thrower at hp 1, delays at most 6 s.
  - Cameo from 24 m back at +9 m/s; taunt 2.3 s.
  - The police go for the player at most once every 6 s.
  - Pickup back to the spec's 35.
  - The W3 thrower still escaped: it spawned behind and needed 7 s to get ahead.

Final run times:
- **First-timer:** every run clears W3 before the bridge. A clean run takes 87-103 s; the average with retries is 108 s. A clean run takes 100-200 stability in all; 13-24 enemy attacks per run, 1-14 of them land.
- **Skilled:** 86-92 s, and the last biker falls around s 2000-2200, before the bridge.

The bot never hits traffic: it rides the route's centre line. It never uses nitro either (agent B). A real first-time player should fail a little more often than the bot.
