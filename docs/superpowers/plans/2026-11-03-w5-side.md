Branch `w5-side` from main 97de880. Brief: `<scratchpad>/w5_brief.md` (binding). Known gaps from

## Interfaces

- **`DriftRival`** (additive setup opts, defaults unchanged so Day 2 is untouched): `"cast"` (StoryCast id, default
  `soi_xam`) or `"look"` (CharacterBuilder rider opts), `"name"` (the label), `"paint"` / `"glow"` (scooter and underglow),
  `"calm"` (no kicks, no blocks: the racing line is `lane`), `"taunts"`. `rival_name` replaces the NAME constant in the
  label and the taunts.
- **`RouteGuide`** (additive): `set_boss(node, name)` (the marker's name text; default "SÓI XÁM").
- **`SideRaceRivals`** (new, `scripts/story/SideRaceRivals.gd`): the 2-3 rivals of a side race. `ROSTER[route]` (names,
  looks, pace), `setup(course, opts)`, `place_grid()`, `go()`, `tick(dt, p_s, p_d, p_v)` (each rival's rubber band: eases
  off ahead, a catch-up push behind, lead cap and a behind cap so every rival stays within about 25 m of the player),
  `standings(p_s, p_done)` (rows sorted by progress or finish time), `place_of_player()`, `near_rival()` (the guide's
  marker: the rival just ahead, else just behind).
- **`SideRace`** (extended): the rivals on the grid, positions on the HUD ("Vị trí 2/4"), the guide marker on the next
  rival (others: edge arrows and radar dots), the podium panel for PODIUM_S after the line, then `finished` with
  `place` / `of` in the result; the rank by time, capped by the place (2nd at best A, 3rd at best B, 4th C). The best time
  is recorded as before. `opts.rivals: false` = the old time trial (tests).
- **`SideDelivery`** (extended): `route_course(id)` (a DriftCourse through the measured Day 2 roads from the start through
  every drop), `RouteGuide` (chevrons, turn panel, radar route), a low gold drop ring instead of the tall beam, the HUD
  "Còn N m" along the route instead of compass words.
- **Radar**: `ViceRadar` draws a blip's optional `"label"` under it (inside the disc only). `StorySideActivities.blips()`
  (the open start spots within RADAR_M, coloured by kind, labels "Đua", "Hạ gục", "Giao hàng", "Võ"),
  `StoryNightSecrets.blips()` (the lit lanterns, "Đèn lồng"); `StoryRoam.blips()` appends both (two lines).
- **Rewards live**: `PlayerBike.refresh_rider_look()` rebuilds the seated rider with the chosen colours;
  `SideRewards.apply_live(roam)` (now, or pending while the story is busy: applied by StorySideActivities when free);
  StoryPhone's Tủ đồ calls it.
- **Tuning**: Difficulty numbers in SideTakedown (aggression, waves) and SideVovinam (attackers, hp, damage) from sloppy-bot
  sims `tests/sim_side_takedown.gd`, `tests/sim_side_vovinam.gd`; `tests/sim_side_race.gd` measures the rivals (distance,
  out of sight, place) and the cost.
