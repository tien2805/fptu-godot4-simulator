# Story Phase B "Ngày 1 và Ngày 4": Design

Date: 2026-10-25. Status: written by Claude from the approved story spec after the owner said "làm tiếp Phase B đi"; decisions taken without a separate review round, as in A2.

Parents:
- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-story-tool-den-design.md`: story spec, sections 3, 4 (Days 1 and 4), 7, 8, 8.4 and 9.3 / 9.7.
- Phase A1 (cinematics), Phase W (weapons, wanted, hit reactions) and Phase A2 (story framework, Day 0): `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-story-phase-a2-framework-design.md`.

## 1. Goal

Days 1 and 4 are playable inside the story, on two new systems that later days reuse:
- unarmed Vovinam combat;
- a horror toolkit.

Days 2 and 3 stay placeholders that the player can sleep through, so Day 4 is reachable. Phase C replaces them.

Out of scope:
- Days 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7;
- shooting, bike combat, sword duels and the Day 7 boss;
- endings and the AI clips.

## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| B-D1 | **Unarmed Vovinam combat** (`scripts/combat/`, story spec 9.3). <ul><li>Controls when unarmed:<ul><li>left mouse: light attack, a 3-hit combo of jab, cross and elbow;</li><li>right mouse: heavy Vovinam kick (đá tạt), which breaks blocks;</li><li>hold Q: block;</li><li>Space while a hostile is within 6 m: dodge-roll with 0.35 s invulnerability (Space jumps otherwise);</li><li>E on a dazed enemy: takedown finisher (a throw or sweep, with 0.3 s of slow motion).</li></ul></li><li>A perfect block (within 0.2 s of the hit landing) triggers an automatic counter-strike, "PHẢN ĐÒN".</li><li>Soft lock-on to the nearest hostile in front of the player.</li><li>Enemies have HP and a stagger meter; a full stagger meter dazes them for 2.5 s.</li><li>Enemy heavy attacks are telegraphed by a red flash 0.5 s ahead and cannot be blocked, only dodged.</li><li>Player HP 100 regenerates after 4 s without damage. Reaching 0 in a story fight fails the mission ("TOANG!", retry from the fight's checkpoint).</li><li>No gore: knockouts, stars, groans.</li><li>In free roam the same moves replace Phase W's simple unarmed punch. Pedestrians still react through the Phase W hit reactions and never fight back.</li><li>Story campus missions force unarmed: the weapon wheel is disabled with the line "Nhiệm vụ này chỉ dùng tay không" (story spec S-D5).</li></ul> |
| B-D2 | **Melee enemies** (`scripts/combat/`). <ul><li>Archetypes:<ul><li>`courier` (Day 1: three couriers, Mũ Đỏ, Mũ Xanh and Mũ Vàng, in helmets);</li><li>`clone` (Day 4: sheep-masked clones, fought in waves);</li><li>`dolly` (Day 4 mini-boss: faster, dodges, flees at 30 % HP).</li></ul></li><li>AI states: approach → circle → telegraph → attack → recover, plus stagger, daze, knockdown and KO.</li><li>At most 2 enemies attack at once; the others circle and taunt.</li><li>Bodies use the PS2 rigged people, with clips from the Quaternius packs plus code poses.</li></ul> |
| B-D3 | **Horror toolkit** (`scripts/horror/`, story spec 9.7). <ul><li>**Flashlight:** [F] toggles it; horror levels have no bike.<ul><li>The battery drains over 90 s while on and flickers below 20 %; 3 battery pickups per level.</li><li>Stalkers see a lit player from 22 m instead of 14 m.</li></ul></li><li>**Hiding spots:** lockers, toilet stalls and under-desk spots; [E] enters and exits.<ul><li>Inside, a low peek camera, a `breath` loop, and `heartbeat` that grows with stalker proximity.</li><li>A stalker that saw the player enter checks that spot (caught). Otherwise the player is safe.</li></ul></li><li>**Stalker AI** (sheep-masked clones):<ul><li>patrol waypoints;</li><li>a 70° sight cone at 14 m (22 m when the flashlight is on);</li><li>hearing: sprint 10 m, walk (Ctrl) 3 m;</li><li>states patrol → suspicious (turn and approach the noise) → chase → search the last known spot for 15 s → patrol.</li><li>A stalker within 1.2 m catches the player: jump scare, then retry from the last checkpoint.</li></ul></li><li>**Scare director:**<ul><li>a budget of 1-2 jump scares per mission, at least 60 s apart;</li><li>also runs flicker events, `whisper_bed`, and heartbeat by proximity;</li><li>with "Giảm hù dọa" (A2-D14) on, each scare becomes a soft sting with no flash and no screech.</li></ul></li></ul> |

## 3. Constraints carried over

- Pure GDScript, Godot 4.7.2, no plugins; CPUParticles3D only.
- No AI-generated images; textures and icons are code-drawn.
- Owner audio and video stay local (`user://music`, `user://sfx`, `user://video`).
- Mixamo raw files stay in the git-ignored `assets/local/mixamo/`.
- Never stage `.import` files.
- No push without the owner's permission.
