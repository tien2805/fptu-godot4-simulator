# Horror polish (Day 4 night and other night scenes) and real music everywhere: Design

Date: 2026-10-25. Status: owner requests in chat, plus the ideas they picked. Builds on Phase B (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-story-phase-b-days-1-4-design.md`).

## 1. Owner requests (verbatim, summarised)

1. Replace the background music: the Day 4 night escape, free roam by day, free roam by night. **"tuyệt đối không xài nhạc code nữa"**: no code-synthesized music anywhere; Claude finds a real track for each slot.
2. Under the desk, if caught: Dolly lowers her face (upside down) into the hiding spot as a jump scare.
3. When Dolly comes near the hiding spot: a strong, continuous heartbeat that builds tension.
4. More jump scares in the night levels and night cinematics.
5. When all 3 fuses are in: the corridor lights flash red in an SOS pattern.
6. Ideas the owner picked from Claude's list:
   - a jump scare per hiding spot;
   - hold breath;
   - music that changes with the situation;
   - an escape sprint after the fuses, plus fake-out scares and Dolly's music-box hum;
   - "thêm ý tưởng cho các màn khác nữa" (ideas for the other scenes too).

## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| H-D1 | **Dolly hunts in the Night Building.** <ul><li>One of the four stalkers becomes Dolly: StoryCast `dolly` look, sheep mask and cream cardigan, slightly faster. The other three stay clones.</li><li>Dolly hums a music-box tune (positional 3D audio, louder as she nears), so the player can tell where she is.</li></ul> |
| H-D2 | **Catch jump scares per hiding spot.** <ul><li>Desk: Dolly's face lowers upside down into the gap under the desk.</li><li>Locker: the door is yanked open, her face pressed to the slats first.</li><li>Toilet stall: her face rises over the top of the partition.</li><li>Caught in the open: her face lunges at the camera.</li><li>Each is about 1.2 s, played as a camera-relative staged moment with a sting from StoryAudio (`violin_screech` / `sheep_bleat_distorted` / `static_burst`).</li><li>With "Giảm hù dọa" on: a soft sting, no face lunge, no screech; a short fade instead.</li></ul> |
| H-D3 | **Heartbeat by the nearest hunter's distance.** <ul><li>Continuous and accelerating, from about 70 BPM at 14 m to about 160 BPM within 2 m; louder as the hunter nears.</li><li>A subtle screen-edge pulse while hidden.</li><li>It keeps going until the hunter leaves 14 m.</li><li>"Giảm hù dọa" caps it at 110 BPM and 6 dB quieter.</li></ul> |
| H-D4 | **Hold breath while hidden.** <ul><li>Hold Shift to hold breath: a 6 s breath bar that refills at 1 s per s when released.</li><li>A hunter within 3 m of the spot hears breathing unless breath is held.</li><li>Running out, or releasing while a hunter is within 2 m, makes a loud gasp: the hunter checks the spot and the player is caught.</li><li>HUD: a breath bar with the plain text "Giữ Shift: nín thở".</li></ul> |
| H-D5 | **SOS lights and the escape sprint.** <ul><li>When the third fuse goes in, the corridor emergency lights turn red and flash SOS (· · · — — — · · ·; dot 0.2 s, dash 0.6 s, gap 0.2 s, letter gap 0.6 s, word gap 1.4 s, looping).</li><li>An alarm siren loops (synthesized SFX are allowed).</li><li>Every hunter rushes toward the player. The lobby doors are open: a final sprint, with the `night_escape` music.</li><li>"Giảm hù dọa": a slower red pulse, no strobe.</li></ul> |
| H-D6 | **Fake-out scares (comic release).** <ul><li>A coat on a hook shaped like a clone that the flashlight catches: a short sting, then a "…cái áo khoác" line.</li><li>A small code-built cat knocks over a bin in a corridor, meows and runs. It is a noise event the hunters hear.</li><li>Placed at seeded spots, at most 1-2 per run.</li></ul> |
| H-D7 | **Jump scares in the other night scenes.** <ul><li>Day 1 stake-out: rustling behind the player, then a fake-out: it is the shiba Lu.</li><li>Day 1 corridor walk: one locker bursts open mid-walk and a sheep mask falls out, before the scripted `cine_d1_corridor` ending.</li><li>Free roam at night (story mode, Day 1 and later), at most one event every 3 minutes, never during a mission:<ul><li>a sheep-masked figure stands under a far streetlight and vanishes when the player gets within 25 m or looks away;</li><li>"Số lạ" texts a photo of the player from behind: "Đẹp đấy {name}.", with the phone buzz;</li><li>rarely, a clone sprints across the road in the bike's headlight.</li></ul></li><li>All of them honour "Giảm hù dọa" and the scare budget (ScareDirector).</li></ul> |
| H-D8 | **Real music everywhere** (no code music). <ul><li>Every music source plays an owner track from `user://music/<slot>.ogg|.mp3`; **a missing file means silence**.</li><li>The synthesized defaults are removed for:<ul><li>MusicDirector styles;</li><li>the CineAudio default cues for cinematic slots;</li><li>the CineReplay stings.</li></ul></li><li>Synthesized SFX stay.</li><li>Slots:<ul><li>`menu`;</li><li>`roam_day` and `roam_night` (free roam on foot and on the bike);</li><li>`stage1_race`, `stage2_parking`, `stage3_classroom`, `stage4_vovinam`, `stage5_canteen`, `stage6_capstone`, `stage_extra_exam`;</li><li>`mission_stakeout`, `mission_chase`, `mission_fight`, `mission_canteen`, `mission_corridor`;</li><li>`night_stealth`, `night_tense`, `night_chase`, `night_escape`;</li><li>every cinematic slot (existing names), plus `wanted_busted`.</li></ul></li><li>Tension (stage 1 speed, capstone boss HP) maps to volume and a gentle low-pass, never to synthesis.</li><li>Stage 4's rhythm notes keep their own 85/110/135 BPM beat, marked by a drum-hit SFX; the owner track plays underneath.</li></ul> |
| H-D9 | **Dynamic Night Building music**, a `NightMusic` node attached by StoryNightEscape and by the standalone NightBuilding run. <ul><li>State from the level's existing API:<ul><li>`stealth` when no hunter is suspicious;</li><li>`tense` when any is suspicious or searching;</li><li>`chase` when any is chasing;</li><li>`hidden` when the player is in a spot: the music ducks to silence, leaving the heartbeat and breathing;</li><li>`escape` once the doors are unlocked.</li></ul></li><li>Crossfades of 1.0-1.5 s, with each track resuming where it left off.</li></ul> |
| H-D10 | **Tests.** <ul><li>Extend `test_horror_toolkit` / `test_night_building` with: per-spot catch scares and their reduce-scares variants, the heartbeat BPM curve, hold breath and gasp, the SOS pattern timing, the alarm and the hunters rushing, fake-outs within the budget, Dolly as a hunter with her hum.</li><li>A music test: every slot resolves to a file or to silence, with no synthesized music path left (grep-style check plus runtime); NightMusic state transitions; crossfades.</li><li>Story night events: the cadence and the no-mission rule.</li><li>Keep `test_horror_perf` / `test_mayhem_perf` < 3 ms.</li><li>Run the light test set plus the affected tests, and the full regression before merging.</li></ul> |
| H-D11 | **Rules:** plain Vietnamese UI text with no emoji; fictional names; no gore; owner music local only; never stage `.import` files. |
