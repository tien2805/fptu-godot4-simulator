# Story Phase C "Ngày 2, 3 và 5": Design

Date: 2026-10-26. Status: written by Claude from the approved story spec after the owner said "làm thêm 1 phase nữa đi". Decisions were taken without a separate review round, as in A2 and B.

Parents:
- the story spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-story-tool-den-design.md` (sections 3.3, 4 Days 2/3/5, 6, 7, 8.3, 8.4, 9.4-9.6);
- Phase B `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-story-phase-b-days-1-4-design.md`, whose systems are reused: melee combat, horror toolkit, StoryChoice, StoryVillainCard, StoryCast, mission runner;
- horror polish and real music `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-horror-polish-and-real-music-design.md`;
- Phase W, whose player weapons, aim camera and hit reactions are reused.

## 1. Goal

Days 2, 3 and 5 become playable, replacing their placeholders, on three new systems:
- bike combat;
- lair gunfights;
- sword duels.

Choice 2 on Day 5 can end the story early with ending C. Day 6 and later stay placeholders; Phase D replaces them.

Out of scope: Days 6 and 7, the speedboat, the final boss, endings other than C, and the AI clips.

## 2. Decisions

| C-D1 | **Bike combat** (`scripts/combat/bike/`, story spec 9.6). <ul><li>While riding: Q kicks left, E kicks right, with a short cooldown and a hit window.</li><li>A kicked biker wobbles, crashes into a comic tumble (no gore), and their bike slides.</li><li>Enemy bikers (Bầy Sói, wolf helmets with LED eyes) pace the player, swerve in to kick, and box the player in.</li><li>Player bike "stability" HP: hits and crashes into traffic lower it; at 0 the player is knocked off (fail, retry from the checkpoint).</li><li>Built on PlayerBike and the RoamTraffic bodies, in FreeRoam at night on the real Nguyễn Xiển road (or the Stage 1 night road if FreeRoam can't host it; the plan picks).</li><li>A short replay `seg_d2_chase` when the last biker falls.</li></ul> |

Campus missions stay unarmed.</li><li>Cover: **hold Ctrl near a low obstacle to take cover** (C is already camera orbit). The player crouches behind it and pops out while aiming.</li><li>Player HP 100 with regeneration after 4 s; at 0 "TOANG!" and retry from the checkpoint.</li><li>Enemy gunmen (guards in black, sheep/wolf/fox masks):<ul><li>states patrol → alert (hear or see) → take cover → peek and shoot (telegraphed muzzle flash 0.4 s before a hit, readable tracers) → flank → stagger → knocked out;</li><li>at most 3 shooting at once;</li><li>accuracy falls with distance and while the player moves.</li></ul></li><li>Hits knock out with stars, no blood.</li><li>Ammo pickups.</li><li>An encounter API like MeleeFight: `GunFight.setup / add_wave / start / checkpoint / retry / stop`, signals `won` / `lost` / `enemy_ko`.</li></ul> |
